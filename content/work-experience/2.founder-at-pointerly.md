## About Pointerly

[Pointerly.io](https://pointerly.io) is an Affiliate Operations Platform for Amazon Associates, creators, affiliate teams, and brands. It unifies smart deep links, bio pages, AI content generation, creator-brand campaigns, and click/commission tracking in a single workflow, instead of spreading links, analytics, and campaigns across multiple tools.

## Responsibilities & Contributions

- **End-to-end product:** Designed and built the platform around revenue workflows (not just “link in bio”), covering distribution, monetization, and reporting.

- **Smart deep links:** Short links with affiliate-tag preservation, device routing (app vs web), and geo/marketplace routing (12+ Amazon stores, plus eBay and Walmart).

- **Creator Connect & automations:** Flows for creators and brands to connect, campaigns, AI-generated content, deal automations, and DMs from comments.

- **Multi-surface product:** Web app, browser extension (Pointerly Connect), and iOS app for approved creators/brands.

## Technologies

- **Web:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Data:** Supabase (PostgreSQL), Drizzle ORM
- **AI & payments:** OpenAI, Stripe
- **Clients:** Chrome extension, iOS (Swift)
