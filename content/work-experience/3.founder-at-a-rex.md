## About A-REX

[A-REX](https://a-rex.org) is a recently launching initiative for verifiable traceability in agricultural supply chains. It combines an open standard for recording, transferring, and verifying agricultural lots with a platform that turns that standard into operational traceability, compliance evidence, and supply-chain automation for producers, processors, and traders.

## Responsibilities & Contributions

- **Product and technical direction:** Define the problem space around verifiable lots and evidence that holds up across partners — not just internal dashboards.

- **Standard + platform:** Shape both the open specification (recording, transfer, verification, governance) and the product surface that puts it to work in real supply chains.

- **Domain continuity:** Build on years of agricultural traceability work (including AgTrace) with a clearer split between shared standards and operable software.

## Technologies

- **Product focus:** TypeScript full-stack web services, REST APIs, React interfaces
- **Domain:** Agricultural traceability, compliance evidence, supply-chain workflows
