## About Evercore

[Evercore.sh](https://evercore.sh) is a platform (founded in 2026, Florianópolis) that provides a regulated trust layer for permanent storage, secure delivery, evidence governance, and enterprise workflow integrations. It is built for legal, finance, healthcare, and supply-chain teams that need auditable proofs — not generic cloud storage.

## Responsibilities & Contributions

- **Product vision and technical direction:** Own commercial positioning and platform architecture around evidence-centric flows (profiles, scopes, approvals, and proof receipts).

- **Permanent evidence layer:** Encryption before upload (AES-256-GCM), versioning and traceability, S3-compatible gateway, data rooms, compliance export packs, and an operational Console.

- **Agentic AI for compliance:** Agents that evaluate documents against business profiles, quarantine violations, and generate reports where findings map to verifiable on-chain receipts.

- **Distributed trust stack:** Arweave (permanence), Hedera Consensus Service (timestamped audit events), and IPFS (content addressing), exposed via Console, Docs, SDK, REST API, and MCP hosts.

## Technologies

- **Platform:** TypeScript, Node.js, React, Encore, REST/MCP APIs, SDKs
- **Trust & storage:** Arweave, Hedera HCS, IPFS, AES-256-GCM
- **Data:** MongoDB, PostgreSQL, Redis
- **AI:** Agents with governed tool calling, business profiles, and proof packs
- **Infra:** AWS, Docker
