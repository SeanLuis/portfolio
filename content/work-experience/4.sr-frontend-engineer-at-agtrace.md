## About AgTrace

AgTrace builds traceability solutions for agriculture, using blockchain to increase transparency and trust across the supply chain. The company develops tools that let producers and consumers access verifiable information about the origin and processing of agricultural products.

## Responsibilities & Contributions

- **Traceability interfaces:** Build and optimize complex UIs with Vue.js/Nuxt.js, Pinia, and Tailwind CSS for the agricultural traceability platform.

- **DApps and smart contracts:** Lead decentralized application development with Solidity, Hardhat, and Polygon, including contract optimization to reduce transaction costs.

- **Supply-chain partners:** Collaborate on traceability projects with Abrapa, Reserva, C&A, and Renner, with chain visibility via QR codes.

- **Networks and integration:** Work with Polygon and Hedera Hashgraph for immutable records and Web3 integration with Viem.

## Technologies

- **Frontend:** Vue.js 3, Nuxt.js, Pinia, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript
- **Blockchain:** Solidity, Hardhat, Polygon, Hedera Hashgraph, Viem
- **Infrastructure:** AWS (EC2, S3), Docker, Git
