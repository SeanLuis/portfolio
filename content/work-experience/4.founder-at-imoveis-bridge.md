## About Imóveis Bridge

[Imóveis Bridge](https://imoveisbridge.com.br) is a founder-built product for Brazilian real-estate agencies: one system for the public site, the operations desk, and finance — covering sale, annual rental, and seasonal rental without fragmenting the property catalog or cash flow across tools.

## Responsibilities & Contributions

- **Product ownership:** Define and ship an operations-first SaaS where site, desk, and finance share one source of truth per agency and brand.

- **Multi-mode operations:** Support sale, annual rental, and seasonal workflows on the same property catalog, with clear handoffs instead of duplicated records.

- **Integrations and AI assist:** Connect payments/channel managers and add bounded AI help for listings, multilingual content, and lead response — without inventing financial actions the operator did not approve.

## Technologies

- **Full-stack:** TypeScript, Node.js, React, REST APIs
- **Data & delivery:** Relational data model for catalog/contracts/finance; cloud-hosted web product
- **Product surfaces:** Branded site, Bridge Desk, finance module, AI-assisted content and lead drafts
