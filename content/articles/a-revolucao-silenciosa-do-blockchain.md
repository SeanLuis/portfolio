Hedera Hashgraph is often framed as a next step beyond traditional blockchains: a public distributed ledger designed for high throughput, fast finality, and energy efficiency — while still supporting smart contracts and tokenized assets.

## Why it matters

Classic blockchains trade off speed, cost, and decentralization. Hedera’s hashgraph consensus (gossip about gossip + virtual voting) aims to deliver:

- High throughput (thousands of TPS in design targets)
- Fast confirmation (seconds, not minutes)
- Predictable, low fees
- Strong fairness properties for ordering

## Practical angle

For product teams, that profile is attractive when you need auditable events, micropayments, or compliance-friendly timestamps without waiting on congested L1s. Hedera Consensus Service (HCS) is especially useful as an immutable event log for evidence and audit trails.

## Takeaway

Hedera is not “Ethereum, but faster” — it is a different consensus and governance model. The interesting question is where hashgraph-style networks fit best: regulated workflows, high-frequency messaging, and applications that need finality as a product feature.
