Blockchain and digital traceability are reshaping global agriculture. The promise is simple: make origin, handling, and custody verifiable end to end — from farm to brand to consumer.

## What changes

- Immutable records of production and logistics events
- Shared visibility across producers, processors, and retailers
- Stronger storytelling for sustainability and ethics claims

## Why it matters now

Consumers and regulators increasingly demand proof, not marketing. Traceability systems that combine UX (QR, dashboards) with blockchain-backed records help close the trust gap between rural production and urban consumption.

## Takeaway

The technological revolution in agriculture is less about novelty tokens and more about operational transparency: auditable data that supply chains can actually use.
